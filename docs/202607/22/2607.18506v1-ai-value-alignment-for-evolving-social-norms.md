## 摘要
AI对齐对于先进AI系统的安全部署至关重要。鉴于价值观和偏好会随时间、文化、社会角色和情境而变化，我们需要更好地理解AI对齐可能带来的长期后果，尤其是考虑到未来个性化AI助手可能无处不在的使用。我们引入了一个灵活且可扩展的数学建模框架，根植于社会物理学，旨在回答在频繁使用AI的假设下，人类群体中社会规范演化的宏观问题。我们的分析部分为解析性，部分为模拟性，使我们能够在多种初始假设下描述长期动态后果。我们强调了价值锁定和规范模式崩溃的风险，这在非自适应对齐公式中尤为突出。除了对齐之外，我们提倡更广泛地采用这类社会物理学模型作为认知桥梁：为通用AI未来的社会技术预见提供快速、严谨且基于定量假设检验的方法，并作为计算成本更高的大规模智能体评估的前驱步骤。

## Abstract
AI alignment is essential for the safe deployment of advanced AI systems. Given that values and preferences change over time, culture, social roles, and context, we need to develop a better understanding of the possible long-term consequences of AI alignment, in particular considering the likely ubiquitous future use of personalized AI assistants. We introduce a flexible and extensible mathematical modelling framework, rooted in social physics, aimed at answering macro-level questions regarding the evolving social norms in human populations under the assumption of frequent AI use. Our analysis is part-analytical, and part-simulation, enabling us to characterize the long-term dynamical consequences under a diverse set of starting assumptions. We highlight the risk of value lock-in, and normative mode collapse, prominently featured in non-adaptive alignment formulations. Beyond alignment, we advocate for the wider adoption of these kinds of social physics models as an epistemic bridge: enabling rapid, rigorous, and quantitatively-grounded hypothesis testing for sociotechnical foresight in general AI futures, and acting as a tractable precursor to more computationally expensive large-scale agentic evaluations.
