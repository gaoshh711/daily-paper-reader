## 摘要
尽管可定制的社交人工智能（如ChatGPT、Grok和Character.ai）日益普及，但我们对用户如何主动塑造社交AI以反映其个人偏好知之甚少。本研究通过新发展的AI个人主义概念，考察了用户（N=169）为何以及如何定制社交AI。通过对开放式回答的反思性主题分析，我们识别了定制的若干动机，包括：（1）增强的实用支持，（2）情感支持或陪伴，（3）信任与可靠性，（4）反驳，（5）量身定制的人类相似度，（6）创造力或趣味性，以及（7）让AI作为自我的延伸。与AI个人主义概念一致，我们的发现表明，对许多用户而言，定制化是人与AI之间的共创过程，被认为能加强支持、自主性、所有权和参与度，可能有助于建立更紧密、更个人化的关系。通过定制化，用户可能将社交AI视为个性化的社交资源，增强其个人主义感、自由感和控制感。我们讨论了这些感知如何可能催生伪自主性，即定制化创造了对强大社交AI系统个体控制的幻觉。

## Abstract
Despite the growing availability of customizable social artificial intelligence (AI), such as ChatGPT, Grok, and Character.ai, we know little about how users actively shape social AI to reflect their personal preferences. This study examines why and how users (N = 169) customize social AI through the lens of the newly developed concept of AI individualism. Through reflexive thematic analysis of open-ended responses, we identified several motivations for customization, including (1) enhanced pragmatic support, (2) emotional support or companionship, (3) trust and reliability, (4) pushback, (5) a tailored degree of human likeness, (6) creativity or playfulness, and (7) having the AI function as an extension of the self. In line with the concept of AI individualism, our findings show that, for many users, customization is a co-creative process between the human and the AI that is perceived as strengthening support, autonomy, ownership, and engagement, potentially contributing to a closer and more personal relationship. Through customization users may come to view social AI as a personalized social resource that increases their sense of individualism, freedom, and control. We discuss how these perceptions may foster pseudo-autonomy, whereby customization creates an illusion of individual control over powerful social AI systems.
